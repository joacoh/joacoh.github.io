## About Me

Hi! I'm **Joaquín Hernández-Yévenes**, an astrophysicist from Chile 🇨🇱 and a PhD candidate at **Swinburne University of Technology** in Melbourne, Australia 🇦🇺. Before moving to Melbourne, I completed my astronomy degree and MSc at the **Universidad de Concepción** in Chile.

My research focuses on understanding how galaxies evolve through the interplay between star formation, supermassive black holes, and galactic-scale feedback, combining multi-wavelength observations with statistical analysis.

### Research & Expertise

- **Multiphase Galactic Outflows:** Studying the kinematics and physical conditions of ionised and cold molecular gas in edge-on starburst galaxies as part of the **GECKOS** collaboration, using integral-field spectroscopy (**VLT/MUSE**), millimetre interferometry (**ALMA**) and infrared photometry (**JWST**).
- **Supermassive Black Holes & Galaxy Scaling Relations:** Probing SMBH demographics and host-galaxy properties across large samples. I developed [**WISE2MBH**](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stae1372), a scaling-based algorithm for estimating black hole masses from WISE catalogues, and contributed to the **ETHER** database supporting **Event Horizon Telescope (EHT / ngEHT)** target science.
