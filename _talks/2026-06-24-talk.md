Academic talk presented at the GECKOS Busy Week. In this talk I presented the results of the homonimus paper describing the ionised and molecular phases of the outflow in the starburst galaxy ESO 484-036, and the early results of PAHs and sodium emission using JWST and VLT/MUSE data. The slides shared here are the same I used for my CoC, since they barely differ.
